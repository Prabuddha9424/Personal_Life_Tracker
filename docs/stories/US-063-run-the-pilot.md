## User story

> **As** the product owner, **I want** a two-week pilot with a small circle of real users, **so that** I learn whether people who are not the builders can sign up and get value without help.

## Acceptance criteria

1. **Given** the pilot guide's checklist, **when** I finish it, **then** every box is ticked before anyone is invited.
2. **Given** 5 to 10 people (PRD question Q1), **when** they use the app for two weeks, **then** the weekly stats show them verified and active in both weeks.
3. **Given** each Monday, **when** I review, **then** I have the numbers, the weekly checks and a fresh backup.
4. **Given** day 14, **when** I hold the check-in, **then** I have answers to the eight questions from every participant, sorted into bugs, wishes and confusions.
5. **Given** the end, **when** I write the results, **then** `docs/pilot-results.md` states which of S1 to S4 were met and the decision to continue, upgrade or stop.
